## 🛡️ Quick Answers for Tough Questions (FAQ)

- **Q: "Is the AI actually reading real patents?"**  
  *A:* *"Yes! We have pre-seeded real Indian utility patents from academic institutions like MIET, and our backend parses the title, abstract, and claims into structured commercial matrices."*

- **Q: "What prevents patent theft?"**  
  *A:* *"Patents listed are already published or granted filings. Furthermore, proprietary claims and source materials remain locked behind the digital NDA gate until approved by the owner."*

- **Q: "How does the platform make money?"**  
  *A:* *"We charge a 5% success commission when a transaction or licensing deal closes through our escrow system, plus premium SaaS subscriptions for enterprise scouts."*

- **Q: "What is the tech stack?"**  
  *A:* *"React + TypeScript + TailwindCSS + Recharts on the frontend, Express + TypeScript + MongoDB on the backend with automated JWT authentication."*
