# SECTION 1 — Homepage and new logo (2 minutes)

### What to do

1. Open **https://patentbaazar.onrender.com**
2. Point to the top-left logo: **IPBridge** with the bridge icon.
3. Read the main headline on the page.
4. Scroll down a little — show 2–3 featured patents.
5. Click **Discover IP** in the menu.

### What to say

> "This is our new brand — **IPBridge**. The logo is clean and sharp on every screen.
>
> The homepage explains what we do: help inventors commercialize patents and help companies discover IP in one place.
>
> These featured patents are loaded quickly — we only pull a few for the homepage so it stays fast."

# SECTION 2 — Discover page (3 minutes)

**Stay logged out** or use the buyer account.

### What to do

1. You are now on **Discover IP**.
2. Show the big search box in the middle.
3. Show the small quick-search buttons below (Edge AI, Healthcare, Battery tech).
4. Point to the text that says how many patents there are — about **80 IP assets**.
5. Scroll down — you see **12 patent cards**, not all 80 at once.
6. At the bottom, show **Previous** and **Next** buttons and text like "Showing 1–12 of 80".
7. Click **Next** — page 2 loads quickly.
8. Click a category button like **AI** or **Healthcare** — the list filters.
9. Click any patent card to open details.
10. Press the **browser Back button** — Discover should load normally with the search bar and list visible.

### What to say

> "This is where companies browse patents. Each card shows an **AI score**, price in rupees, and a short description.
>
> Before, the page tried to load all patents at once. That was slow. Now we load **12 at a time**. You use Next and Previous to see more. The page feels much faster.
>
> We also fixed a bug where sometimes the screen went **blank** when you pressed Back from a patent. That works properly now.
>
> Titles are partly hidden until the buyer gets approval — that protects the inventor's IP."

### Optional — AI search

1. Type something like: **smart home privacy IoT**
2. Click the blue **Search** button with the sparkle icon.
3. Show results ranked by AI.

> "Buyers can also describe what they need in plain English, and AI finds the best matches."

# SECTION 3 — List IP button (3 minutes)

This part shows how the **List IP** button works for different users.

---

### Part A — Logged in as BUYER

1. Click **Login** → use **buyer@patentbridge.com** / **password123**
2. Click **+ List IP** in the top menu.

### What to say

> "A company buyer wants to list a patent. But buyers are here to **buy** IP, not list it.
>
> So instead of a confusing signup page, we now show a clear message: you are logged in as a **buyer**. To list patents, you need an **inventor account**.
>
> You can go back to Discover, or switch to an inventor account."

---

### Part B — Logged in as INVENTOR

1. **Log out** → log in as **owner@patentbridge.com** / **password123**
2. Click **+ List IP** again (or go to Dashboard → **List new IP**).

### What to do

3. The **listing form opens automatically** — you do not land on a marketing page.
4. Walk through the form slowly:

| Part of form | What to point out |
|--------------|-------------------|
| Top | Title says "List intellectual property" and explains admin reviews before it goes live |
| Patent details | Patent number, title, abstract — normal labels, easy to read |
| Price | Type a number — it formats in Indian style (e.g. 50,00,000) and shows ₹ below |
| Listing options | Two boxes — **Sale** or **Royalty licensing** — click to select |
| Bottom | **Submit for review** button stays visible while you scroll |

### What to say

> "For inventors, List IP goes **straight to the form**. No extra clicks.
>
> We redesigned this form. Before, labels were all capitals and the submit button could disappear off screen. Now it has clear sections, Indian rupee formatting, and the submit button stays at the bottom.
>
> When they submit, AI creates a commercial summary, and admin checks it before it appears on Discover."

*(You do not have to submit a real patent in the demo unless you want to.)*
